===== what to read off this =====
If the largest benchmark closure is SMALLER than the traversal, no closure can contain
an inlined copy of it, and `core-native` is reached by a real call in that binary --
which is what the FFI arm does too. The inlining advantage the objection describes
would then not be present to be subtracted.
