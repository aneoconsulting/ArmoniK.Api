# decode, decode-read: core-ffi's decode, the FSM family since FIX-PLAN D24 (ak_fsm_begin_<Root> + one ak_fsm_next_<Root> per further

# decode-read: the decode, then every field of the host's value read (the core grid's decode direction, CAMPAIGN 4.0).

# C and D (and their framed and callback twins) decode the response with the FSM family (D24); B decodes with prost, E with core-native.
# Bf, Cf, Df, Ef: the same cells on the framed send path (T1 option 3; ak_client_set_framed is called once at open, not per call).
# c/P5.3, c/P5.4: U1-unary, one upload of M5 (encode + Upload; the response is empty and decoded by nobody).
# d/4MiB, d/16MiB: U2-stream, one client-streamed upload in 2 MiB chunks (B/C/E: open, a send per chunk, recv, free, destroy).
# The counts do not depend on the h2 variant (stock or h2-batch): h2 is inside the core, below every counted entry point.
